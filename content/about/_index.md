Hello there! I'm Dishant Langayan, based in Ottawa, Canada, and this is my personal blog where I like to share my thoughts and opinions on anything technology related.

Over the past fifteen years my career has taken many different paths with [Solace](https://solace.com), the company I still work for:

* 2011 - I started as a professional services consultant working on several investment banking projects, developing applications, designing and architecting solutions, and teaching customers about Solace products.
* 2017 - I moved on to managing Solace's customer training, and with a two-person team we delivered training to our customers across the globe.
* 2018 - I got involved in building enablement programs for our sales teams and even helped sell our first [Solace Cloud](https://solace.com/products/platform/cloud/) deal.
* 2019 - I grew my team to build onboarding programs for employees.
* 2020 - Well, everyone knows what happened in 2020. I was busy building our company's first certification program and launching our [Solace Academy](https://training.solace.com).
* 2021 - I focused on building our sales enablement, from sales plays to launching Solace Hub, built on SharePoint, for company-wide communication and content sharing.
* 2022 - I was promoted to VP and took on managing our Business Operations team, where I played a key part in updating our internal processes, tools, and integrations, along with building our very first Deal Desk and RFP team.
* 2024 - I was given the opportunity to move back to where it all began and lead the global Professional Services team, transforming our services business from majority time and materials to 85% recurring, subscription-based revenue.
* 2026 - I was promoted to SVP. Over the last two years we've changed the way we deliver services to our customers, moving from project-oriented work to outcome-focused, advisory-style consulting with multi-year engagements.

Throughout my career, the developer inside me hasn't gone away, and I still write code and build applications and integrations, sometimes for fun and sometimes to improve company operations.

When I'm not in front of a computer, I love to [travel](https://www.tripadvisor.ca/TravelMap-a_uid.4D9E15C7214416E91BF484EFD77D3B4B) (there is so much to explore in this world) and maybe get some time on my guitar, which I learned during the pandemic.

I hope you enjoy the content I publish and share. You can get in touch with me through [my LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dishantlangayan/).
